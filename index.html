02 · About

Bridging hardware and cloud

I love building software that bridges the physical and digital worlds — from hardware integration and embedded firmware to mobile companion apps and the backend APIs that tie it all together. I've been writing software for 30 years across a wide range of domains, and I now run my own consultancy, LKA Technology, providing senior engineering services to teams that need experienced help without a full-time hire.

Earlier in my career I spent eight years at Performance Software developing embedded avionics display software to DO-178 and DO-330 airworthiness standards — work that instilled the discipline safety-critical development demands.

What makes my background unusual is the breadth: equally comfortable close to the hardware, building a companion app in Swift or Kotlin, or designing the backend API that ties it together. For companies building connected hardware with companion applications, that combination is hard to find in one person.
